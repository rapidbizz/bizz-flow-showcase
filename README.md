# Bizz Flow

> Multi-tenant staff management SaaS — by RapidBizz.

Staff management for shops and small teams: attendance, roles and billing in one place, with a portal for each level.

## What it does

- **Three portals** — Super Admin, Admin / Shop Owner, and Staff
- **QR attendance** — staff check in by scanning a QR code
- **GPS radius** — check-ins verified within a 100 m radius
- **Device lock & anti-fraud** — attendance tied to the staff member's device
- **Billing** — plans and billing for each tenant

## Who it's for

Shop owners and small teams who still track attendance on paper or in chat groups.

## Demo

The `demo/` folder holds small, self-contained examples in TypeScript, JavaScript and CSS. They are illustrative examples only, not product code.
